## 🧩 Project Overview

This project analyzes **2022 retail transaction data** to evaluate overall business performance.

The analysis focuses on:

- Revenue trends and seasonality  
- Product category performance  
- Regional sales contribution  
- KPI development  

It enables stakeholders to make **strategic and data-driven decisions**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify top-performing product categories  
- Analyze monthly revenue trends and seasonality  
- Evaluate regional sales contribution  
- Measure key performance indicators (KPIs)  
- Discover revenue growth opportunities  
- Enable strategic business decision-making

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### 📅 Revenue Trends
- March recorded the highest revenue (₹1,825,897)  
- November recorded the lowest revenue (₹1,462,714)  
- Strong Q1 performance indicates seasonal demand  

### 🛍 Product Performance
- “Set” category generated the highest revenue  
- Bundled fashion products show strong demand  
- Promotions can further boost high-performing categories  

### 🌍 Regional Analysis
- Maharashtra contributes the highest revenue share  
- Indicates strong regional demand  
- Expansion opportunities exist in similar markets  

### 🧾 Customer Purchase Behavior
- Average Order Value is ₹748  
- Higher units than orders indicate multi-item purchases  
- Strong potential for cross-selling strategies
